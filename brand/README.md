# brand/

The secondhand brand, in the ui-factory form. Status: exploring. Round 1 ([board](https://claude.ai/artifact/95hea28zWVkFy9afpMZfY8)) tried wordmarks and a yellow-and-red flower system; on 2026-11-14 19:22 Ben parked the logo and asked for wider directions with white as the main color, a QOVES-like calm in its own style, and open accent colors. Round 2 is running from his inspiration photos. At 19:44 he set the method: build the brand modularly, picking each piece (color, gradient, shadow, headline, text and label faces, icons, shape, emphasis, buttons, density) on its own. The picker is the [brand kit](https://claude.ai/artifact/NdqVRvvD43fFPnhxDFbiFZ); his saved picks become `tokens.css`. Nothing here is Decided.

Facts that hold whatever direction wins: Clofie (all 18 weights) has only 103 glyphs, no accents or × ° ″ – •, so it can set headlines we write but never listing titles or measurements; the Clofie files are a personal-use demo (commercial use and modification need Dealita Studio's license); red #E63339 on white is 4.29:1 and yellow #FED734 on white is 1.40:1.

- **`constitution.md`: how anything secondhand makes looks and sounds, in any medium and language, with ends, codes, rules and checks. Start here.**
- `codes.md`: round 1 codes, superseded by the constitution's Article 4
- `tokens.css`: the only place values live
- `coder-card.md`: read before any UI edit

- `anti-pillars.md`: round 1 list, superseded by the constitution's Article 13
