## Shared card (prepended to every prompt except the default sample)

secondhand is an iPhone app for students and casual sellers. You photograph a used thing once; it lists it on eBay, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari and Facebook Marketplace, answers buyers, and asks you only when a decision needs you. Calm and fast; no chatbot; decisions are buttons.
Brand, from the attached swatch and type sample: white pages, black text. The one color event is "the field": a sky-to-lime gradient with fine curved white lines, used once per view, behind the thing that matters, always with black text on it. Lime also marks the money you keep, as a highlight band behind the lower part of the figures. Item photos lead every card. The one action that moves things forward is a black pill; the next action is an outlined pill; everything else hides under a gray "···". Marketplace logos instead of their names. Wordmark: lowercase "secondhand" in the serif shown. Text in the sans shown, regular weight, with medium only on buttons; no bold, no uppercase labels.
What to do instead of common defaults: real photos of used items in daylight instead of illustrations or 3D; one textured field instead of gradients everywhere; space to group things instead of boxes inside boxes; plain sentences ("Jamie offered $38. You asked $45.") instead of stats, badges or taglines.
Do not resize or post-process the image.
