Questions

What if it sells on two sites at once?

It comes down everywhere else as soon as one sells. If two buyers pay in the same minute, you pick which sale to keep, and we show what cancelling the other costs on that site.

Will my photos be changed?

Only if you choose it. True color uses no AI at all. Touch-ups are labeled, tags and flaws are never touched, and your original is always kept.

Do I need an account on every site?

No. Connect the ones you have. It posts where you're connected and tells you what you'd keep on each.
