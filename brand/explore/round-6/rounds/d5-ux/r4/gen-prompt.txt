- It isn't true phone scale (390 points wide). Text you can't read at phone size, or buttons that look 1.5 times too big, both fail.
- A logo is wrong or generic, for example Facebook drawn as a store icon.
- It shows any Article 13 tell (brand/constitution.md), or any of these never-candidates: an invented name; purple plus forest green on lavender and cream; Poppins with Lora; spaced uppercase labels; a handwritten script tagline; torn paper edges, sparkle and burst marks; a stock photo of a smiling person; "Used" badges and heart buttons on item cards; a generic icon row or arrows-to-boxes flow diagram.
