# Brand codes

Status: round 1 agent proposal, superseded as a direction (Ben parked the logo and widened the color brief at 19:22). Kept for its boundaries, which hold for the yellow and red if they return. Ben picks on the [brand board](https://claude.ai/artifact/95hea28zWVkFy9afpMZfY8). Nothing here is Decided until he does.

Each code is a signature asset with its exact value and a boundary.

1. **Wordmark: "secondhand", all lowercase, the o drawn as a clock with a red second hand** (board option 20). Clofie SemiBold, outlined to SVG, tracking −0.035em then hand-kerned. Boundary: never under 16 px tall; below that use the flower icon. Never recolor the hand to anything but center red. Ben's angles: "your second hand" (it does the work) and the second hand of a clock (speed).
2. **The flower: five flower-yellow petals around a center-red dot.** The palette's own names describe it. Boundary: on white it always sits on black (the camera button, the app icon) or is large enough that the red center carries it; never as a small yellow-only shape on white.
3. **Flower yellow as a highlight behind the number you keep.** One highlight per screen. Boundary: never as text, lines or icons on white (1.40:1).
4. **Center red only for dots, alerts and the second hand.** Boundary: no red text under 24 px (4.29:1), no red buttons (white on red fails; black on red is ugly and reads as error).
5. **Two-luminance headlines** (QOVES): the second phrase at 45% black instead of a weight change. Boundary: 24 px and up.
6. **Mono uppercase labels for every source, unit and status** ("measured", "estimated", "you said", "SOLD RANGE · N=41").
7. **One round yellow price dot per screen at most** (borrowed from the sticker direction), for the number that matters: a sale, a deal verdict.

Sources: Ben's colors, fonts and QOVES type rules are in his 2026-11-14 18:56 message in the brand thread; the QOVES measurements are in `qoves-ui-reference.md` on his Mac.
