Each item is marked READ (observed in the source text), READ excerpt, ABSTRACT (asserted by the abstract only) or secondary. Nothing here is a verdict on using a method; that is in [spec/engines/adaptation.md](../../../spec/engines/adaptation.md).


Marks: READ = seen in source text (PDF text or fetched page). ABSTRACT = abstract or search-highlight excerpt only. Labels: sourced / inferred / not-found. No verdicts or recommendations. Numbers from ABSTRACT items were not checked against the full paper.

## 1. eBay Best Offer (READ, full text)
- Backus, Blake, Larsen, Tadelis (2020), "Sequential Bargaining in the Field: Evidence from Millions of Online Bargaining Interactions", QJE 135(3):1319-1361. https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjaa003 ; NBER WP 24306 https://doi.org/10.3386/w24306 ; PDF https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/stadelis/qjaa003.pdf [sourced]
- Data: eBay Best Offer, US single-unit listings created 31 May 2012 to 1 June 2013. Sample is about 88.4M listings with BIN price $0.99-$1,000, 18M with an offer, 25.5M threads, 1.2M sellers, 4.7M buyers. Max 3 offers per party, 48h expiry.
- Table I: mean list price $95. Sale price / list 83% (incl. BIN). Bargained price / list 0.727. About 80% of listings get no offer. Thread agreement 0.454. First buyer offer averages $86.60 = 60.8% of list. 1.66 offers per thread. One-third of interactions end in immediate agreement.
- Table II/text: bargained price / list 0.70 collectibles, 0.79 electronics. Agreement 0.596 media, 0.317 electronics. First offer/list 0.575-0.660 across categories. Other Table II cells were garbled by pdftotext and not read cleanly.
- Photos: more photos mean faster first offer (Fig. VII, Table VI).
- Limits stated: footnote 9 cautions against reading cross-category differences as asymmetric information; eBay Motors excluded (fn 25).
- NBER public dataset: https://www.nber.org/research/data/best-offer-sequential-bargaining [sourced]. Files: Codebook.xlsx, anon_bo_lists.csv.gz (98M listings, 1 May 2012 to 1 Jun 2013; listing price, reference prices, category), anon_bo_threads.csv.gz (offers, counteroffers, timing, status, prior transactions of parties), bargaining_data.zip, code. NSF #1629060. Page says cleared for public release by eBay and available for research; PII removed; asks users to cite the paper. No formal licence text found. Contact

## 2. Auctions vs posted prices; consumer search at eBay (READ, full text)
- Einav, Farronato, Levin, Sundaresan (2018), "Auctions versus Posted Prices in Online Markets", JPE 126(1):178-215. https://doi.org/10.1086/695529 ; https://web.stanford.edu/~leinav/pubs/JPE2018.pdf [sourced]
  - Data: all eBay listings 2003-2009 plus matched listings. Auction prices were within 5% of matched posted-price sales in 2003, discount over 16% by 2009 (not from lower reserves). Posted-price sale rate fell 40.3% to 25%. Auction sale-probability premium about 8% (2003), 11% (2009). Attributed to falling relative demand for auctions, stronger in differentiated categories. Limit: model "very parsimonious".
- Dinerstein, Einav, Levin, Sundaresan (2018), "Consumer Price Search and Platform Design in Internet Commerce", AER 108(7):1820-1859. https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20171218 ; http://web.stanford.edu/~leinav/pubs/AER2018.pdf [sourced]
  - Data: eBay browsing data; structural estimation on one product (Halo Reach). After search redesign prices fell about 5-15% for many products. Own-price elasticity about -10 (-10.64 in table). Ranking-sampling parameter 0.80 (SE 0.14). A 10% price cut raises consideration-set entry 0.08 to 0.11 (27% more likely). Top-rated-seller at $37 equals non-TRS at $35.21. Limits: cannot capture equilibrium responses; results differ for heterogeneous categories.

## 3. Time to sell with right-censored data
- Kaplan & Meier (1958), JASA 53(282):457-481. https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1958.10501452 [ABSTRACT]. Estimate assumes lifetime independent of potential loss time; "deserves careful scrutiny".
- Cox (1972), JRSS B 34(2):187-202. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1972.tb00899.x [ABSTRACT]. Hazard = covariates times arbitrary baseline; conditional (partial) likelihood.
- Allison (1982), "Discrete-Time Methods for the Analysis of Event Histories", Sociological Methodology 13:61-98. https://doi.org/10.2307/270718 ; https://statisticalhorizons.com/wp-content/uploads/Allison.SM82.pdf [READ excerpt]. Assumes censoring independent of hazard; person-period pooling gives a binary regression.
- Singer & Willett (1993), J. Educational Statistics 18(2):155-195. https://doi.org/10.3102/10769986018002155 [ABSTRACT]. Discrete-time hazard fitted by standard logistic regression; 3,941 special educators.
- Genesove & Mayer (2001), QJE 116(4):1233-1260. https://doi.org/10.1162/003355301753265561 ; https://realestate.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/323.pdf [ABSTRACT]. Boston condos 1990-97. Loss-facing sellers set asking prices 25-35% of the loss higher and get 3-18% higher selling prices. A 10% gap between prior price and market value cuts weekly sale hazard 3-6%.
- Carrillo (2012), Int. Economic Review 53(1):203-234. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-2354.2011.00677.x [ABSTRACT]. MLS Charlottesville VA 2000-02. Full-information counterfactual: posting and transaction prices fall 4.0% and 4.4%, time on market rises about 7 days. Working paper (https://www2.gwu.edu/~iiep/assets/docs/papers/Carrillo_IIEPWP2012-11.pdf) treats withdrawn or expired listings as censored and uses Kaplan-Meier [READ excerpt].

## 4. Price indexes from sparse transactions
- Bailey, Muth, Nourse (1963), JASA 58(304):933-942. https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1963.10480679 [ABSTRACT]. Same-property price relatives as a regression; more efficient than other ways to combine relatives; gives standard errors.
- Case & Shiller (1989), "The Efficiency of the Market for Single-Family Homes", AER 79(1):125-137. http://academics.wellesley.edu/Economics/case/PDFs/Efficiency.mar1989.pdf [READ excerpt]. BMN = OLS of change in log price on time dummies (+1 at second sale, -1 at first). Weighted repeat sales = 3-step GLS: BMN, regress squared residuals on constant and sales interval, weighted regression; motivated by variance growing with interval. Finding: yearly price changes followed by same-direction changes the next year.
- IMF/ILO Handbook on Residential Property Price Indexes, ch. 6. https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9789279259845/ch006.xml [READ excerpt]. Matched-properties approach, useful when resales are few (para 6.2). Case-Shiller WLS corrects heteroskedasticity growing with interval; Calhoun (1996) adds squared interval (para 6.13).
- FHFA HPI technical description. https://www.fhfa.gov/document/hpi-technical-description [READ excerpt]. Repeat sales controls composition without estimating attribute prices; used because GSE data lack characteristics.
- Mei & Moses (2002), AER 92(5):1656-1668. https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2F000282802762024719 [citation only]. Per Mandel (2009, https://www.brmandel.com/uploads/3/2/4/5/3245755/mandel_artasaninvestment_aer2009.pdf) [secondary]: Sotheby's/Christie's NY paintings 1950-2000, about 5,000 repeat-sale pairs; median real paintings return 2.6%.
- Goetzmann, Mamonova, Spaenjers, NBER WP 20440. https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w20440/w20440.pdf [ABSTRACT/secondary]. Mei-Moses index explains 0.64 of return variation vs 0.59 for Goetzmann 1993.
- Ginsburgh/Pesando-style survey, JEP "Measuring Returns on Investments in Collectibles". https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/pdf/10.1257/jep.13.4.193 [READ excerpt]. Repeat sales uses only items sold at least twice; hedonic uses all sales but constrains return to a constant over the period.
- Stated limits of repeat sales from secondary excerpts: resale selection (items resell only if they gained; Goetzmann 1996; Korteweg, Kraussl, Verwijmeren 2013); about 20% of paintings drop out of auction records (Goetzmann 1996); annual-aggregated indexes understate volatility.
- Collectibles applications: AAWE WP 142 https://wine-economics.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/AAWE_WP142.pdf (8,582 repeat pairs, 4.1% real GBP annualized 1900-2012) [ABSTRACT]; Fogarty AAWE WP 70 https://wine-economics.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/AAWE_WP70.pdf (14,102 Australian wine auction sales 1988-2000; repeat sales gave significantly higher returns than hedonic or hybrid; hybrid most efficient) [ABSTRACT]; Dimson & Spaenjers (2011) JFE stamps [citation only]; sneakers thesis using StockX data https://scholarship.claremont.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=3748&context=cmc_theses [snippet, not peer reviewed].

## 5. Shrinkage for small groups
- Efron & Morris (1975), JASA 70(350):311-319. https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1975.10479864 ; https://www.jhanley.biostat.mcgill.ca/bios602/MultilevelData/EfronMorrisJASA1975.pdf [READ excerpt]. 18 players, first 45 at-bats of 1970 predicting the rest of the season; also toxoplasmosis rates and a chi-square simulation. Sample-mean total squared error 17.56 vs 5.01 for Stein (efficiency 3.50); Stein closer for 15 of 18. Limited-translation (s=0.8): max component risk 4.60 to 1.52 keeping 80% of savings. Limits: equal variances needed; arcsine transform used; individual-component risk can rise (Clemente).
- Gelman et al., Bayesian Data Analysis 3rd ed., ch. 5 "Hierarchical models" (5.1 parameterized prior; 5.3 conjugate hierarchical models; 5.5 eight schools; 5.7 weakly informative priors for variance parameters). TOC https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/book/BDA3.pdf [ABSTRACT/TOC]. Rat tumor example: beta-binomial, 70 historical groups with sample mean 0.136 and SD 0.103 giving moment-matched Beta(1.4, 8.6); current 4/14 gives Beta(alpha+4, beta+10); hyperprior p(alpha,beta) proportional to (alpha+beta)^-5/2 [excerpt via https://github.com/wargile/Bayesian/blob/master/BDA05.txt and https://www.pymc.io/projects/examples/en/latest/generalized_linear_models/GLM-hierarchical-binomial-model.html; secondary copies of the book text]. Chapter text says weakly informative priors matter for hierarchical models fit to a small number of groups.

## 6. Learning under change with forgetting
- Garivier & Moulines (2011), ALT 2011, LNCS 6925:174-188. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-24412-4_16 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.3415 [ABSTRACT]. D-UCB and SW-UCB for abrupt changes at unknown times; both match a regret lower bound up to a log factor; simulations beat EXP3.S. Regret constants not seen.
- Raj & Kalyani (2017), "Taming Non-stationary Bandits: A Bayesian Approach", arXiv:1707.09727. https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.09727 [READ excerpt]. Discounted TS multiplies past success/failure counts by gamma each step before update; gamma=1 recovers standard TS. Derives only the two-armed suboptimal-pick probability; regret bounds left as future work.
- Russo, Van Roy, Kazerouni, Osband, Wen (2018), "A Tutorial on Thompson Sampling", Found. Trends ML 11(1):1-96. https://djrusso.github.io/docs/TS_Tutorial.pdf [ABSTRACT]. Includes a nonstationarity section.
- Exponential forgetting in recursive estimation:
  - Ljung, "Recursive Identification Algorithms" (report), https://www.rt.isy.liu.se/research/reports/2001/2366.pdf [READ excerpt]. Weight lambda^(t-k); memory horizon about 1/(1-lambda); tracking-vs-noise trade-off in the choice of forgetting factor; RLS is optimal tracking only if parameter variation covariance is proportional to P.
  - Guo, Ljung, Priouret (1992), "Tracking performance analysis of the forgetting factor RLS algorithm", CDC. https://doi.org/10.1109/cdc.1992.371639 [ABSTRACT]. Tracking error of order sqrt(mu) + gamma/sqrt(mu), mu = 1 - lambda.
  - Campi (1994), IEEE Trans. Signal Processing. https://doi.org/10.1109/78.330351 [ABSTRACT]. Error splits into a drift term proportional to memory length and a noise term inversely proportional to it.
  - Kulhavy & Zarrop (1993), "On a general concept of forgetting", Int. J. Control. https://doi.org/10.1080/00207179308923034 [ABSTRACT]. Result close to classical exponential forgetting, regularized by prior information.

## 7. Calibrated prediction ranges
- Lei, G'Sell, Rinaldo, Tibshirani, Wasserman (2018), JASA 113(523):1094-1111. https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2017.1307116 ; arXiv 1604.04173 [ABSTRACT]. Finite-sample marginal coverage; full and split conformal; locally varying length extension.
- Romano, Patterson, Candes (2019), "Conformalized Quantile Regression", NeurIPS 32. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/file/5103c3584b063c431bd1268e9b5e76fb-Paper.pdf [ABSTRACT]. Adapts to heteroscedasticity; shorter intervals than other conformal methods.
- Tibshirani, Barber, Candes, Ramdas (2019), "Conformal Prediction Under Covariate Shift", NeurIPS 32. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/file/8fb21ee7a2207526da55a679f0332de2-Paper.pdf [READ excerpt]. Weighted conformal needs likelihood ratio dP_test/dP known or estimable from unlabeled test covariates; assumes Y|X unchanged; coverage at least 1-alpha.
- Gibbs & Candes (2021), "Adaptive Conformal Inference Under Distribution Shift", NeurIPS 34. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/0d441de75945e5acbc865406fc9a2559-Paper.pdf [ABSTRACT]. Long-run coverage frequency regardless of data-generating process, by re-estimating one parameter online; tested on two real datasets.
- Angelopoulos & Bates (2023), "Conformal Prediction: A Gentle Introduction", Found. Trends ML. https://doi.org/10.1561/2200000101 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2107.07511 [READ excerpt of sec. 4.5: covariate-shift weights, quantile formulas 33-37; covers distribution shift and time series].

## 8. Our own actions change what we observe
- Horvitz & Thompson (1952), JASA 47(260):663-685. https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1952.10483446 ; https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~brian/905-2008/papers/Horvitz-Thompson-1952-jasa.pdf [ABSTRACT]. Unequal selection probabilities; unbiased estimation including variance.
- Dudik, Langford, Li (2011), "Doubly Robust Policy Evaluation and Learning", ICML. https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/3104482.3104620 [ABSTRACT]. Accurate when either reward model or past-policy model is good; reward models carry bias, past-policy models carry variance.
- Joachims, Swaminathan, Schnabel (2017), "Unbiased Learning-to-Rank with Biased Feedback", WSDM. https://doi.org/10.1145/3018661.3018699 ; https://www.cs.cornell.edu/people/tj/publications/joachims_etal_17a.pdf [READ excerpt]. Propensity-weighted ERM (Propensity SVM-Rank) against position bias; propensities from click models or a small randomization pilot; shown on an operational search engine.

## 9. Engagement as a demand signal
- Yuan, Chen, Mathieson (2011), "Predicting eBay listing conversion", SIGIR 2011. https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/2009916.2010188 [READ excerpt]. Models P(sale) from listing attributes and click data; page views, watch and impression counts used as dynamic factors. Table: heuristic model accuracy 64% at listing time, 80% with demand observations; GBDT 82% at listing time; logistic on GBDT score plus runtime signals 88%. Ratio of listing price to median price of recently sold similar items is highly correlated with conversion (Fig. 1). Separate watch-count effect not reported in the excerpt.
- Zifla & Wattal, "Understanding IT-Enabled Social Features in Online Peer-to-Peer Businesses for Cultural Goods", JAIS. https://aisel.aisnet.org/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1863&context=jais [READ excerpt]. Etsy seller-level data, 12,879 monthly obs (Jan-Dec 2015), 4,902 for the Jul-Dec favorites subset, 3SLS on log sales. Favorites coefficient in the sales equation -0.0913 (direct, p<0.01), in the status equation +0.0956; net effect of a 10% rise in social features +0.744% sales. Seller-level, not item-level.
- Kim, Ahn, Park (2026), Behaviour & Information Technology. https://doi.org/10.1080/0144929x.2026.2662414 [ABSTRACT]. Secondhand marketplace multimodal model, accuracy 79.2%; number of favourites had comparatively less impact than seller trust, description, price.
- Wu, Hu, Hong, Liu (2018), "Turning Clicks into Purchases", SIGIR. https://doi.org/10.1145/3209978.3209993 [ABSTRACT]. Etsy search logs with clicks and purchases, 4 weeks.
- Etsy interaction-type embeddings, arXiv 1812.04407. http://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.04407 [ABSTRACT]. View, favorite, collection, cart, purchase treated as distinct intent signals; experiment treatment +4.1% CTR, +0.20% conversion, +0.31% checkout (not statistically significant per the authors).

## 10. Seasonality
- Cleveland, Cleveland, McRae, Terpenning (1990), "STL", J. Official Statistics 6(1):3-73. https://www.math.unm.edu/~lil/Stat581/STL.pdf [READ abstract and intro]. Y = T + S + R by loess; handles missing values; robust to outliers; period any integer greater than one.
- Dekker, van Donselaar, Ouwehand; Hyndman group, "A state space model for exponential smoothing with group seasonality". https://robjhyndman.com/papers/GSI.pdf [READ excerpt]. Group seasonal indices: seasonal indices estimated from all products in a group; needs one year across several series instead of several years of one series; simulations show when it beats Holt-Winters. (Author attribution inferred from the citations in the excerpt; title page not seen.)
- Birrell, Lin, Steel (2016), "Univariate and multivariate approaches to seasonal adjustment of aggregate series of different lengths". https://doi.org/10.3233/mas-150349 [ABSTRACT]. Multivariate approach improves parameter accuracy, especially for short to medium series.
- Eurostat, "The seasonal adjustment of short time series" (KS-DT-05-002). https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/3888793/5832357/KS-DT-05-002-EN.PDF/54bdfe54-f3b3-49cb-a0e5-9edcc64199ff [ABSTRACT]. Adjustment quality worsens as series shorten (5 y vs 10 y vs full); X-12 degrades less than TRAMO-SEATS.
- Spiliotis et al. (2019), PMC6776316. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6776316/ [READ excerpt]. States seasonality shrinkage is considered effective for short, noisy series (cites refs 51, 52 there, not traced).

## 11. Price drops and time on market in posted-price resale
- de Wit & van der Klaauw, "Asymmetric Information and List Price Reductions in the Housing Market". http://papers.tinbergen.nl/10038.pdf ; published version https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0166046213000240 (journal inferred from the PII prefix as Regional Science and Urban Economics) [READ excerpt]. Dutch NVM administrative data 2005-2007, timing-of-events duration model with competing risks (sale vs withdrawal). List-price reduction coefficient delta_s = 0.606, so sale rate +83%; withdrawal delta_w = 0.366, so withdrawal rate +44%. Identification needs buyers not to anticipate the exact moment of the cut.
- Merlo & Ortalo-Magne (2004), "Bargaining over residential real estate: evidence from England". https://lbsresearch.london.edu/id/eprint/4204/ [ABSTRACT]; stylized facts quoted in the home-selling-problem paper https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2014/retrieve.php?pdfid=830 [READ excerpt]: 780 properties, June 1995 to April 1998; 77% never changed list price, 18% once, 4% twice, 1% three times; reductions infrequent and large, triggered by lack of offers, larger the longer on market; first offer relative to list is lower the longer on market; about a third of negotiations end in separation.
- Knight (2002), cited in de Wit excerpt [secondary]: houses with large cuts from initial list take longer to sell and sell lower.
- Kryzanowski & Wu (2022), "Signaling effects of recurrent list-price reductions on the likelihood of house sales", JFIR. https://doi.org/10.1111/jfir.12308 [ABSTRACT]. Joint frailty model; time-varying negative effect of recurrent cuts on sale likelihood; sold houses sell at a higher sold/last-list ratio.
- Huang, Luo, Xia (2019), "Invest in Information or Wing It? A Model of Dynamic Pricing with Seller Learning", Management Science. https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2018.3197 [ABSTRACT]. Used-car dealership panel; value of learning during selling $203 per car, initial assessment adds $139, dealer net profit per car about $1,150.
- Online-resale (C2C) markdown studies: none found with item-level cut and time-on-market estimates (see Not found).

## 12. Promotion and ad slots
- Blake, Nosko, Tadelis (2015), Econometrica 83(1):155-174. https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA12423 ; NBER WP 20171 https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w20171/w20171.pdf [READ excerpt of WP]. Returns from paid search are a fraction of conventional non-experimental estimates; brand-keyword ads no measurable short-term benefit. Non-brand test: ads stopped in about 30% of US DMAs (68 test DMAs, 142 control) for 60 days, difference-in-differences; effect on sales small and statistically insignificant; new and infrequent users positively influenced; frequent users account for most spend; short-term ROI negative.
- Moshary (2025), "Does Sponsored Search Advertising Augment Organic Search? Evidence from an E-Commerce Platform", Management Science. https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.04792 [ABSTRACT]. Field experiment blocking all sponsored search for 3% of visitors; sponsored ads steal from organic listings and reduce total transactions intermediated; still profitable for the platform because ad revenue exceeds the commission loss.
- Joo, Shi, Abhishek (2024), "Do Sellers Benefit from Sponsored Product Listings? Evidence from an Online Marketplace", Marketing Science. https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2021.0293 [ABSTRACT]. Mobile app field study with experimental and natural variation; consumers prefer organic over sponsored in top positions and are indifferent in lower positions.
- Abhishek, Jerath, Sharma (2024), "The Impact of 'Retail Media' on Online Marketplaces: Insights from a Field Experiment", ISR. https://doi.org/10.1287/isre.2022.0188 [ABSTRACT]. Flipkart; effects vary by category (electronics: sponsored worse than displaced organic; clothing/footwear: better); raising the sponsored fraction by about 10 points did not change total clicks or conversions.
- eBay Promoted Listings, seller-run trial, ChannelX (2019). https://channelx.world/2019/02/ebay-promoted-listings-have-a-significant-effect-on-salesexperimental-results/ [READ excerpt]. Just over 15,000 BIN/Best Offer listings per week, 13-week baseline then alternating halves; sell-through up on average 27% in 8 of 9 weeks; R^2 of 43% between ad rate and sell-through. Disclosure: eBay refunded fees; trade press, not peer reviewed.
- Webinterpret blog (2023). https://webinterpret.com/en/blog/ebay-promoted-listings-ad-rates [READ excerpt]. About 1,000,000 UK-seller listings, 50% promoted, Q4 2019 and Q1 2020; impressions 4-5x, visits +60-70%, sales +35% (Germany) and +54% (USA); some cannibalization of organic sales noted. Vendor blog, not peer reviewed.
