## What this changes in earlier notes

- Row 2 narrows "photo count matters more than quality": the figure is a correlation on resale profit across categories, not a sale-probability effect.
- Row 1 adds effect sizes the earlier note lacked, and confirms views and price outweigh predicted image quality in that data.
- Row 4 conflicts with the earlier 0.3733 figure for Kaggle top RMSLE (0.3875 in Mercari's own post; the earlier value came from a different source, so both may be right for different leaderboards).
- Row 3 names the platform (Xianyu) the earlier note left blank.
- Row 6: eBay's own help gives no sell-through formula, so earlier use of sold / (sold + active) rests on third parties.
