# How do we post on Poshmark (US) so it sells, and what belongs there?
Researched 2026-11-14 by Ben (Claude Code research helper). Feature: list.

Feeds the Poshmark adapter. Posting route, delist and ban risk are in [crosspost-and-delist.md](../crosspost-and-delist.md) and are not repeated here. Marks: *observed* = I opened the page today, *asserted* = a source or my memory claims it, *inferred* = my reasoning. Pages were read through a fetch summarizer, so quoted words are its excerpts. The fetch budget ran out early ([limits](#what-i-searched-and-couldnt-reach)), so many numbers are *asserted* and need one check each.

## 1. Short answer

1. **Cover photo in 3:4 portrait.** Since 2026-03-25 Poshmark shows covers in 3:4 across search and feed, and crops square photos *observed*. Shoot portrait with the whole item inside a centered safe zone.
2. **Complete structured fields.** Poshmark names brand, size and condition as what search matches on, and keyword stuffing as a negative *observed (via VAR reporting Poshmark's blog)*. Category and color feed the filters *asserted*.
3. **Offers do the selling.** List above the target, then use Smart Sell or Offer to Likers with a shipping discount. Poshmark says Smart Sell listings are "60% more likely to sell within the first 7 days" *observed (Poshmark's claim)*.
4. **Ship fast and never cancel.** Average ship time shows on the profile, a cancel rate over 3% in 90 days can restrict the account, and the October 2026 seller program rewards 2-day shipping with a search filter *observed*.
5. **Stay active.** Poshmark says active, responsive closets "are prioritized in discovery" *observed (via VAR)*. Sharing now only moves a listing in the "Just Shared" sort *observed (via VAR)*.
6. **Price at $15 or more.** Under $15 the fee is a flat $2.95 *asserted*, which is 21% of a $14 sale and 59% of a $5 sale *inferred*.
7. **Keep it under 5 lb.** One buyer-paid $6.49 label covers 5 lb. Heavier costs the seller $5 or $10, and 15 lb is the cap *observed*.

Biggest difference from the other platforms: Poshmark is one flat world. One fee rule, one label price, no local pickup, no auctions in the catalog, and a category list that stops at fashion, home, pets, toys and small electronics. Anything big, heavy or outside that list does not go here.

## 6. Title and description conventions

**How search reads them.** Poshmark says complete brand, size and condition let search match buyers, and keyword stuffing hurts *observed (via VAR, 2025-10-13 blog)*. Its Q3 2026 advice: low impressions, refresh the title or description; impressions but no clicks, change price or cover; clicks but no offers, add description or sizing detail *observed*. Title and description are both searched *asserted (memory)*.

**Title formula** *agent proposal, from seller convention*: `Brand + Style name + Item type + Key detail + Color + Size` and `NWT` at the end only when tags are attached. No emoji, no "L@@K", no brand names that are not the item's brand.

- Clothing: `Lululemon Align High-Rise Pant 25" Black Size 6 Leggings` (56 characters)
- Non-clothing: `Le Creuset Stoneware Mugs Set of 2 Marseille Blue 12 oz` (55 characters)

**Description template** *agent proposal*: 4 to 7 short lines.

1. One line saying what it is, in the buyer's words.
2. Condition and every flaw, plainly ("small pull on left cuff, shown in photo 6").
3. Measurements laid flat: pit to pit, length, waist, rise, inseam. Poshmark buyers ask for these in comments when they are missing *asserted (seller folklore)*.
4. Material and care.
5. Fit note ("fits like a small").

Leave out: phone numbers, emails, other apps, "trades" (see section 13), hashtag walls, and brand lists ("like Aritzia, Reformation"), which Poshmark treats as stuffing *inferred from the stuffing statement*.

Tone: friendly and plain. Comments are public.

## 7. Tags, categories and structured data

- **Category and size** drive the filters most buyers use (size, brand, category, color, price, condition) *asserted*. A wrong category hides the item from filtered searches *inferred*.
- **Brand** must be the real brand. Listing a non-brand item under a famous brand is a policy violation *asserted*.
- **Colors**: fill both when the item has two.
- **Style tags**: use all 3, real style words only ("Y2K", "Western", "Minimalist") *agent proposal*.
- **Hashtags in descriptions**: old seller habit, no Poshmark statement found. Skip *inferred*.
- **Condition**: pick the honest value. Sellers worry it will feed disputes *observed (via VAR)*, so the adapter maps our condition grade down, never up.

### Under Ben's touch-up rule

- **What we meet at capture.** Every Poshmark tip above is a capture choice: plain wall or floor, window light, item centered, portrait *inferred*.
- **Crop the adapter makes** *agent proposal*: cover at 3:4, 1200 by 1600 px, with the whole item inside the centered 1200 by 1200 square. That survives both the portrait crop and any square surface. Other photos also 3:4.
- **Order** *agent proposal*: 1 full front, 2 full back, 3 brand tag, 4 size and fabric tag, 5 detail (print, hardware, sole), 6 and on one photo per flaw, last a tape-measure shot. 8 photos by default, up to 12.
- **Touch-up**: exposure, white balance, straighten, crop. Nothing else.
- **What the rule stops.** White cut-out backgrounds and banner or collage covers, which sellers here use. We cope by guiding a pale, plain surface at capture and putting "NWT" in the title and condition field. Poshmark tried white cut-outs itself and pulled them, so a real plain background is no handicap *inferred*.

## 11. Shipping and local pickup

- **Label.** Prepaid USPS Ground Advantage, 2 to 5 business days, emailed at sale. Buyer pays $6.49 for up to 5 lb *observed*.
- **Heavier.** Upgrade before shipping: $5 for 5.1 to 10 lb, $10 for 10.1 to 15 lb, seller pays. Nothing over 15 lb on one label *observed*.
- **Packaging.** Ground Advantage must not go in Priority Mail boxes. USPS charges $5, taken from earnings *observed (via VAR)*. Free Ground Advantage supplies can be ordered in the app *observed (via VAR)*.
- **Hazmat.** Nail polish, perfume and aerosols cannot ship. The seller now pays the full $50 penalty *observed (via VAR)*.
- **Handling time.** Ship within 2 days to qualify for the seller program *observed*. After 7 days unshipped the buyer can cancel *asserted (memory)*.
- **Local pickup.** None. No cash, no meetups. Every sale ships *asserted*.

## 12. The algorithm

What Poshmark has said, in the order it lists them (2025-10-13 blog, *observed via VAR*; 2026 posts *observed*):

1. **Listing quality.** Complete brand, size, condition. No keyword stuffing. Clear title, competitive price, good photos.
2. **Activity.** Log in, list new items, keep the closet current.
3. **Responsiveness.** Answer comments and offers fast. Use Offer to Likers and price drops. "Active, responsive closets are prioritized in discovery."
4. **Reliability.** Ship on time, avoid cancellations. The profile shows items sold, last active and average ship time.
5. **Sharing.** Counts as activity and refreshes the listing in the "Just Shared" sort.
6. **Not factors.** Following, and sharing other closets.
7. **2026 additions.** Listing impressions for sellers (April). A reliable-seller search filter (October).

Seller folklore, all *asserted*: share 3 times a day at set hours; "share jail" after thousands of shares (Nifty's docs cap its own tool at 8,000 shares a day "for account safety"); new listings get a "Just In" lift so delete and relist every 30 to 60 days; list daily at 8 pm. None has a Poshmark source.

Relist versus edit *inferred*: Poshmark's advice is to refresh the title, description, cover or price of a stale listing. A delete and relist is now allowed, but it loses the likers that offers go to. Edit first. Relist only when a listing has no likes.

I could not confirm today's default search sort. Poshmark has changed search and rolled changes back at least once *asserted* (two VAR titles, unopened).

## 15. Adapter rules (agent proposal)

All *agent proposal*. Section in brackets.

1. **Refuse** `furniture`, `large_appliances`, `tools`, `instruments`, `auto_parts_vehicles`, fragrance, nail polish, aerosols, used makeup, and anything over 15 lb [11, 13, 14].
2. **Warn** on `avoid` types, on any price under $15 ("flat $2.95 fee, bundle it?"), and on weight over 5 lb ("you pay $5 or $10") [4, 11].
3. **Title**: `Brand Style Item Detail Color Size`, at most 80 characters, no stuffing, `NWT` only with tags [5, 6].
4. **Description**: the 5-line template, at most 1,500 characters, measurements always, flaws always [6].
5. **Structured fields**: brand, category to subcategory, size, 2 colors, condition (never graded up), 3 style tags, original price (retail lookup, user confirms) [5, 7].
6. **Photos**: 8 by default, up to 12 of 16, all 3:4 at 1200 by 1600 px, item inside the centered square, order from section 8. Touch-up note: light, color, straighten, crop only [8].
7. **Price**: list at target divided by 0.85, whole dollars, minimum $3 [9].
8. **Offers**: turn on Smart Sell with minimum = the user's floor. If Smart Sell is missing, send one Offer to Likers at 10% off with the smallest shipping discount once there are 3 likes. Each later offer must go 10% lower again, so send at most two before the floor [9].
9. **Refresh**: day 14 with impressions low, rewrite title; day 21, new cover or 10% price drop; day 45 with zero likes, delete and relist. Never relist a listing that has likes [12].
10. **Sharing**: no automated shares. One optional user-approved bulk share a day [10, 13].
11. **Shipping**: buyer pays the $6.49 label. Ask for weight at capture. Tell the user to ship within 2 days and not to use Priority boxes [11].
12. **Delist**: void open offers first, per the [crosspost note](../crosspost-and-delist.md#poshmark-details).
13. **First run**: read the live form's limits and overwrite section 5 [5].

Human taps, not automatic: approve the post; confirm brand, size and condition; accept or counter any buyer offer below the floor; any price drop; delete and relist; buying a label upgrade; cancelling an order.
