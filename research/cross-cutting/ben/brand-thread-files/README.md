# Files from the brand threads, kept for their history

These were made by the "Set the secondhand brand identity" and "Build the brand system for everything" threads and lived only in the project's shared files. They are the working record behind [brand/constitution.md](../../../../brand/constitution.md): the Today mockups Ben reacted to (v3, v4, v6), the generators and HTML that drew them, the color-blind simulations, the dark-mode audit, the post and deck templates, and the first inputs.

The rules in `brand/` win where these differ. Kept: every file not already in the repo. Left out: copies of the demo item photos and site logos (the app holds its own in `app/assets/`) and a GIF that duplicates `today-v6/camera-flow.mp4`. No font files. Their notes are kept as `.txt`, as written: their links point at where they first lived, so they are a record, not live docs.
