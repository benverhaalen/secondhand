# Website scene photos (mock)

Generated with Seedream 5.5 through OpenRouter, from [shots.json](shots.json) by [gen.py](gen.py), run by the `web scene photos` workflow. They exist to judge the photography direction for the signed-out website. They are mock placeholders under brand Article 8.4: never shown as a real listing, a real customer or a real room. The items are Ben's real ones from `assets/demo-items/real/`, attached as references so the item stays true.

Each scene comes in two variants that differ on one axis: `person` (one person doing the act, face turned away) and `empty` (the same moment with no one in it). Article 8.2 bars people from item photos. Whether the website's scene photos may show people is open with Ben.

`runs.jsonl` has one row per call: the time, the seconds, the prompt length and the usage.
