## Notes
- Counter-evidence: eBay and vendors claim listing is already fast (30 s AI generation, 50% fewer steps). Pitch should say the listing draft is solved-ish for eBay; the remaining pain is cross-platform, messages, pricing, shipping (inferred, not measured).
- Fee floor inferred: effective take 10-20%+ (eBay ~13.6%+$0.40, Poshmark 20%, Mercari 10%, Depop ~3.3%+$0.45, FB 0-10%).
- Low-value items (<$15) sell at 8.6% in 30 days and Poshmark charges $2.95 flat: inferred, time cost often exceeds proceeds. A "worth listing?" appraisal step is supported by this.
- 12.2% 30-day sell-through is a window rate not a "never sells" rate.
- NOT reached/found: Poshmark help fee page (robots); Mercari help pages; Facebook Marketplace ToS on automation; Mercari ToS; any independent time-and-motion study; time spent on "is this still available" messages and pickup scheduling (no data found); share of resellers who crosslist; Vendoo/List Perfectly/Nifty user counts; Poshmark/Mercari sell-through; eBay lifetime unsold share; surveys of reasons for not selling (hassle/scam fear) — only the 2019-20 clutter/Mercari items surfaced and the Mercari page I tried had moved; Census/SSA household storage penetration; donation rates; eBay earnings-call transcript for AI listing claim.

## Counter-evidence / calibration
- FTC online shopping median loss is ~$130 and category total ($434M, 2024) is small next to $12.5B all fraud and $15.9B 2025; investment and imposter scams dwarf shopping. Fraud is a minority of transactions; no per-transaction rate found.
- UK banks refunded 78% of purchase-scam losses; the TSB/Lloyds style numbers come from banks promoting warnings (bank-specific, small samples).
- Pew: only 12% had a shopping problem in the past year.
- Social-media scam shopping is mostly ads to fake stores, not peer-to-peer resale; do not conflate.
