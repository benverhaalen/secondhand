Researched 2026-11-14 by Ben (Claude Code agent, concept thread). Feature: cross-cutting.

Researched 2026-11-14. These notes cover what shipped resale, identification, measuring, pricing, crosslisting, messaging and alert products already do, so each screen of secondhand can point to a precedent.

## Evidence marks

- **observed**: I opened the source page this session and the claim is on it. Many pages were read through a summarising fetch, so "observed" means the page states it. I did not see the live app UI myself.
- **asserted**: the source states it without showing it (for example, a press release, or a user review describing their experience).
- **inferred**: my reading or synthesis.
- **UNOPENED**: the page came up in search, but the shared web-fetch budget (100 per hour, shared across agents) ran out before I could open it. Any claim marked UNOPENED comes from my prior knowledge or the search snippet, and should be verified before it is relied on.

Budget note: the fetch budget ran out partway through. I later opened and upgraded StockX, Chrome Shopping Insights, eBay saved searches, the Meta Muse address incident and Plaid. The remaining UNOPENED rows are Mercari Smart Pricing, Merlin, Pinterest Lens, iNaturalist, OfferUp, Calendly, Keepa, Vinted alerts, Vendoo onboarding titles and the Mercari importer, and those are the leads to verify first.

## Flow 1. Capture an item for sale from photos

| Reference | What it does (concrete UI detail) | Job for us | Evidence mark | Source URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| eBay magical listing (photo-first, 2023 onward; visual ID update announced 11 Nov 2026) | Three steps: take a photo, AI fills title, description, category and item specifics, then the seller reviews, corrects and publishes. **Avoid:** eBay's mandatory AI, camera-permission gate and one-screen-per-section correction. Show the whole draft on one scrollable sheet with low-confidence fields flagged, and always offer "type it myself".

| Vinted sizing change backlash | A UK report of user fury at a Vinted size change ("chaos"). | Grailed / ThredUp measurement display | I couldn't load listing pages (Grailed returned a client error). Output a SmartSizer-style annotated photo plus Klothes-style diagrams. Store tagged size, normalised size per channel and measured values as separate fields, phrased eBay's way ("Tagged M; fits like S; pit-to-pit 20 in").
**Avoid:** presenting AI measurements as exact (SmartSizer's 8 in variance), and fixed measurement sets that users can't trim. Say "approximate, within about ½ in" and offer a "measure again" consistency check.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Pinterest Lens visual search guides, adjacent | From prior knowledge: after a camera search, tappable "guides" (refinement chips) narrow what it saw, for example a style or colour. | **Interaction**: chips for narrowing an identity ("Nike Air Max" then "90", "97") | UNOPENED | https://techcrunch.com/2017/05/09/pinterest-adds-visual-search-guides-to-its-lens-and-a-new-cto/ |

**Avoid:** eBay's confident-but-wrong single answer and its silent removal of the feature. Never auto-commit an identity that drives price and category without confirmation.

|---|---|---|---|---|

Show eBay-style sold median and range with the comps tappable, and power the "fast" end with eBay or Mercari auto-markdown down to a floor the user sets.
**Avoid:** a bare AI number with no comps (eBay's $12-for-$100), and mixing listed and sold prices without saying which (Marketplace uses listed). Always label "sold" vs "asking".

**Avoid:** slow or unreliable alerts (eBay community threads, the Vinted bot ecosystem). Show "last checked 2 min ago" so users trust silence.
