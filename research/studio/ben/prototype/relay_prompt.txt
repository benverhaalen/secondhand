Re-lay prompt. Agent proposal. Tried 2026-11-14 on Seedream 5.0 Pro and Nano Banana 2.1.
Inputs in order: 1 the cover photo, 2 the blank template (the 1:1 or 3:4 outline), 3 the
print or fabric close-up, 4 the other side. {garment} is shirt, sweatshirt or pants.
{ironing} is one of the three blocks below.

----
Image 1 is a real photo of a second-hand {garment} lying on a floor. Image 2 is a blank
layout guide: a dashed outline showing where a {garment} sits in the frame. Images 3 and 4
are a close-up and the other side of the same {garment}, for reference only.

Return image 1 with the {garment} laid flat and straight inside the outline from image 2,
squared to the frame. Do not draw the outline.

{ironing}

The neck tag is tucked down inside the collar so it cannot be seen. Do not show or draw any
tag or label text.

Nothing else changes. The same floor. The same {garment}: same color, same proportions of
body, sleeves and collar, same print at the same size and place with every letter exactly
as in image 3, same fading and wear. No studio look, no new light.
----

{ironing}, hand-smoothed:
Remove about half of the wrinkles, as if the seller smoothed it by hand before shooting.
Clear creases and folds remain. It is the same worn fabric, not new.

{ironing}, ironed:
This is how this exact {garment} would look if you took it and ironed it, then laid it
down: flat, with only the faint soft creases that ironed fabric still has. It is the same
worn fabric, not new, and not a flat graphic.

{ironing}, freshly ironed (the only wording that visibly moved the result):
Freshly ironed means: the fabric lies smooth and flat across the whole body and sleeves.
No crumpling, no fold lines, no ripples, no creases across the chest or stomach. At most
two or three very faint soft lines remain. The fabric still looks like the same soft,
slightly faded, worn fabric with its real weave; it is not new, not glossy, not a flat
graphic.

Why the tag line is there: without it both the re-lay and the in-place ironing prompt drew
a tag with invented text. Tucked in, nothing is invented, and the real tag is shown in its
own untouched photo.
