Design specification only. No app code changed. Source baseline: `1107a97a`, read on 2026-11-15 and rechecked for the 10:31 feature and wiring audit. The person is holding an iPhone, often with one hand. Camera is used whenever they add or inspect a thing. First launch is used once, or resumed after an interruption. They want a usable draft or their account ready, without learning our internal machinery. The costly mistakes are losing photos, asserting an identity without evidence, sharing credentials with the wrong party, importing unwanted listings, and mistaking capture for permission to post.

Success journey: launch, sign in, skip or finish each setup decision, reach Today; open +, pick an intent, allow camera or choose photos, capture one useful photo, leave early or finish the guidance, reach the matching result with the photo and answers retained. Back from the result cannot reopen a completed capture. Back from Today cannot reopen completed sign-in or onboarding.

### Defaults for every control

Use `app/src/ui/press.tsx` (`Press`) through the parts. Touch down responds immediately, drag away cancels, touch up commits. `MOTIONS.press` is a spring, not a fixed duration; the current part uses 150 ms in and 300 ms return parameters. Reduce Motion gives an opacity dip. Disabled controls announce disabled and do nothing. All targets reach 44 by 44 without overlapping neighbors.

`app/src/ui/motion.ts`: `sheet` 300 ms entering, `select` 200 ms leaving or changing a selection, `fieldsFill` 150 ms, `cameraOpen` 300 ms, `stageDone` 200 ms, `foundDrop` 250 ms, `foundFly` 300 ms, `cloudPoint` 200 ms, `shutter` 150 ms. Native push/pop and keyboard movement use the platform transition, with the frontend's panel timing as the visual budget; do not fake a duration token that motion.ts does not contain. No custom motion on scrolling, typing, tab switching or data being read.

A light haptic fires once when a photo is actually retained or an explicit change is saved. One success haptic when capture is durably finished. One warning haptic when a new actionable failure appears. No haptic for navigation, opening a permission prompt, scrolling, each streamed fact, retry taps before an outcome, or repeated renders. Follow the frontend's commit rule over vendor examples that tick every selection. Never double-fire shutter and fact-found feedback for the same shot. Existing `FoundPop:261` fires on mount; the builder must prevent it duplicating the retained-shot event.

A short choice uses `Sheet`: content height, no detents, scrim/Close/Back dismiss, returns to the unchanged step. A tall choice uses `RouteSheet`: content height up to the existing maximum with a scroll body and pinned act, no detent API. Neither currently provides native medium/large detents. Do not describe either as a native detented sheet. Detents and a hosted login sheet need the explicit shared addition below.

## Camera capture session

**Purpose:** collect usable evidence about one thing for the chosen intent, then open its draft, valuation or search result.

It is one full screen flow above the tabs, not a fifth tab and not an Item editor. Its steps change inside its own navigation stack. The one thing that stands out is the real thing inside `CaptureFrame`; the shutter is the next action. On permission denial, the recovery action becomes the focal point.

### Entry, exit, persistence and one-way doors

- Normal entry is + > the chosen job. Use the touched control's actual origin with `CircleStage` and `cameraOpen` 300 ms. The code currently initializes `circle` as open (`93`) and uses `usePlus` (`114`), so do not claim it already animates from every entry point. A direct link with no visible origin enters by crossfade; it has Today underneath. Buy's existing camera shortcut uses its own icon origin and `find` intent.
- Camera hides the entire tab bar and Messages. Close returns to the exact originating tab, stack and scroll offset. At a step root, iOS has explicit Close, no fake horizontal pop gesture and no swipe-down dismiss on the viewfinder. Android Back runs the same close logic. Within pushed type/answer steps, Back and edge swipe return one step. Back never removes retained photos or undoes a server event.
- Before any photo or changed answer, Close leaves immediately. After work exists, Close opens a `Sheet` headed `Leave capture?`: `Save for later` primary, `Keep taking photos` secondary, and `Discard capture` as a quiet disclosure into a destructive confirmation. Saving retains the session and queued uploads; discarding requires a second explicit tap and cannot discard an already-finished item. A failed local save stays visible with Retry and Keep taking photos; never silently drop files. This is a short decision, not another route subject.
- Finish is a one-way door only after photos and answers have a durable local session and an acknowledged or safely queued finish operation. Close the capture stack and land on Item researching/review (`sell`), What's it worth (`worth`), or Search results (`find`), preserving the actual capture/result IDs. Do not hard-code the demo `it_draft`, jacket product or blank search. Native Back from the result goes to the originating tab, not camera, auth, or New.
- Deduplicate Finish and upload retries by capture ID. One tap starts the finish state; duplicate taps cannot create two items. Background jobs may continue while the person leaves. Later additions use Add photos from Item, not Back into a completed capture.
- Persist accepted/rejected/queued photo IDs, current requirement, skipped requirements, selected type, intent and answers. On interruption, pause camera and microphone, retain session; foreground rechecks permissions and resumes at the same unresolved step. Never auto capture on return from Settings or background.

**Purpose:** establish the person's account and optional operating preferences, then open Today.

The next action on each step resolves just that setup decision. This is a full screen setup flow with a stack, not a scrolling account dashboard, feature tour, or camera entry. Sign-in is mandatory in the phone app; every later setup stage can be skipped. The bottom bar is absent until Today.

### Step pattern, progress and navigation

Root launch resolves session and saved setup state before showing a screen. Signed out opens sign-in; signed in but unfinished resumes the next unresolved stage; finished opens Today. Never flash Sign in under an existing session. Guards must use real session state, not `signed=1` (`onboarding.tsx:20-22`).

Use one `Screen` per step with `wash={false}`, `Nav`, one heading and one topic in `Stack`/`Section`, then a single forward act. This is a full screen task, so it does not inherit the tab's wash, inbox, summary or floating +. Root `Nav` shows the wordmark. Deeper `Nav` shows Back; do not hand-draw another wordmark. Native stack Back and edge swipe return to the previous setup stage with selections and text intact. The current `setStep` implementation does not supply edge-swipe history.

Use stable major-stage progress in secondary `Txt`: Sign in, Your sites, Link sites, Import, Sale emails, Pickups, Notifications are stages 1 through 7. A variable link substep says Link 1 of 2 in that stage, rather than changing the major denominator. No selected sites: mark Link and Import skipped without flashing blank pages. No eligible Gmail integration: omit that stage and present the remaining actual stage count consistently from the initial saved plan. Do not show fake progress toward stages the person cannot reach. Back revisits a resolved stage for editing but does not silently unlink, unimport or unsend a code.

After sign-in, remove auth screens from the back stack: first one-way door. Finishing Notifications, including Not now, persists completion and replaces the setup stack with Today: second one-way door. Neither edge swipe nor Android Back can reopen those completed states. Before that door, Skip is local to a step; optional steps after it still appear. Closing the app preserves progress. No automatic sign-out, rollback or discard on Back. Setup resumed from a Today top slot is a focused optional task and dismisses back to Today, rather than replaying account authentication.

#### Repairs applied to the redesign

- **Session, O02/O14.** Resolve launch from real session and `GET /v1/me`; store refresh credentials in the device's secure store, not fixture state or ordinary note storage. Refresh once through the shared session layer; a revoked session returns to Sign in with the last provider first while preserving completed server setup. A later action requiring sign-in is a modal over its source and returns there after success; it does not replay first launch. Provider tokens/codes remain outside UI logs. The auth service and its persistence must be proven separately from the screen.
- **Capability and risk, O06.** The link sheet shows the actual site logo, access scope, one capability line and one real account-risk line before the sign-in action. An unsupported posting route is shown as such; no Claim that we connected to Depop's partner API. After return, the site's `LinkRow` says linked/waiting/needs you based on account data; a linked-but-paused account is not ready to post. A quiet `ListRow` Manage sites at the setup recap leads to You > Accounts after setup, rather than embedding management here.
- **Import, O07/O08.** Differentiate no listings from preview unavailable, and no purchases from purchases unsupported. If there is only a purchase count but no purchase-record preview, do not invent selectable purchase rows: show an explicit Import purchases choice only when the backend can support that scope. Bring in selected sends stable external listing IDs, then records the import job. Show accepted records/failed records separately and retry failed IDs only. A possible match to an existing item is retained as a `merge_items` decision for Today; it does not merge because the person imported. A `ListRow` Review possible matches goes to those Today decisions after completion, not a forced merge detour during sign-in.
- **Progress and notifications, O11/O12.** `done_steps` records that a stage was resolved; it does not itself distinguish linked from skipped or granted from denied. Persist the actual account/preference/permission result as well. If skip outcome needs a new field, label it a contract addition, not an assumed backend behavior. Final completion is recognized only after every visited or explicitly skipped stage is resolved; local queued updates may finish offline, then reconcile on reconnect without asking permissions again. Keep denied OS permission distinct from failed push registration and preferences-write failure: the OS cannot be changed by Retry registration. Existing alert preferences are not discarded when permission is denied. A next-setup reminder may name a skipped useful step, but no reminder reopens mandatory sign-in or undoes the finish door.

## Builder acceptance path

This is a usable design handoff, not a rendered or integrated UI. Source reading, requirement trace and the prose lint were done; no simulator, permission prompt, motion recording, fresh independent visual review or backend integration was run for this document. New patterns and the email/motion conflicts remain proposals for reconciliation.

The builder should demonstrate this path and its alternatives, in light/dark, largest Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion:

1. Cold launch with unknown session shows no sign-in flash. Sign in by each method; cancel providers; reject and resend a code; go Back; reopen after interruption. Successful auth cannot be reached again with Back.
2. Select zero, one and several sites. Cancel a link, finish 2FA, time out one link while another succeeds. Preview zero, one and forty records, untick across pages, import partially, retry only failures. Skip Gmail, choose Ship only or save a public spot, deny notifications, complete to Today. Back cannot re-enter completed onboarding.
3. From each intent in +, open Camera, deny/allow access, return from Settings, choose a limited-library photo, cancel picker, change type, retake, Skip, step Back and early Finish from one photo. Capture failures never become kept-photo counts. No photo cannot finish.
4. Go offline after a retained photo, add another, save and leave, resume, reconnect, and finish once. Photos and user answers survive; no duplicated item; result intent and actual IDs are right. Back from each result returns to the origin, never a consumed capture.
5. Exercise uncertainty, None of these, I don't know, duplicate match and valuable-item prompts. Dismiss each without inventing an answer. Try Clean up, Enhance without LiDAR, failed studio job, and Use original. Try microphone denial, keyboard dismissal and backgrounding; no surprise capture resumes.
6. Scroll recap/import and setup both directions, re-tap the current tab only after exit, interrupt sheet motion, and check safe areas and long labels. Customer navigation and release deep links never reach dev tools.

Passing source checks alone is insufficient: the full moving journey, real state changes, permission recovery and one-way doors must be observed before the implementation is called usable.
