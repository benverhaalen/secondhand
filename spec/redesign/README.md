## 1. Start here

Build one family of places: Today asks for a decision, Sell finds an owned item, Buy finds something wanted, and You opens the right personal detail or setting. Each deeper page answers one question about one subject. Keep the full action set through focused destinations rather than removing capabilities to reduce clutter. This is a reconciliation proposal, not an approved visual design or evidence that the app is wired. Sources: Ben's supplied 10:27, 10:30 and 10:31 words; `product/ui-constitution.md:37` Articles 1, 2 and 4; `tabs.md` Shared tab scaffold and You.

The authority order is the UI constitution, frontend standard, brand constitution, `spec/pages.md:266`, then the lanes. Ben's current request replaces the old multi-purpose You dashboard in pages §6.16. Other departures from those sources are called out below. A lane citation such as `objects.md` means `spec/redesign/objects.md`. Existing means found in source, proposed means a destination or contract still to build, and pending pattern means Ben has not approved it. A handler file is not evidence of a live service or a working control. Sources: user request; `lists.md` Feature and wiring audit conventions; `objects.md` Feature and wiring audit added for 10:31.

The complete first build journey is launch into Today, open New from its visible center button, capture or import one item, review that same item, choose Plan, inspect Post, approve once, see each site's actual result, return through Sell, then complete Ship or Pickup and inspect its payment. The buying journey is Buy search, Search, Listing, approved Offer or external purchase, then the saved Want or bought item. You must reach every setting and detail without regaining a dashboard. These journeys exercise shared containers before further pages are converted. Sources: `flows.md` Builder acceptance path; `sheets.md` Builder acceptance walk; `objects.md` Acceptance journeys for the later builder; `lists.md` Acceptance walks for the builder and reconciler.

## 3. One family of scaffolds

### Rules shared by all six

Use `app/src/ui/layout.tsx:24` `Screen`, `:56` `Section`, `:65` `Stack`, `:70` `Line`, `:75` `Row`, `:86` `List`, `:98` `Card`, and `:108` `Grid`. The six scaffold names here describe compositions, not six existing exported components. All visual parts below come from `app/src/ui/parts/`. Never invent a `Valuation`, `SettingsScaffold` or `NativeSheet` import that the kit does not export. Sources: `product/primitives.md:1`; `objects.md` Shared object-page scaffold; `lists.md` The two scaffolds and exact kit vocabulary.

Geometry is gutter 20; Nav to title 8; title and the first related subject in one Section 16; separate topics 32; related rows/controls 12; tightly related facts 8. Use Section/Stack nesting to express those relationships, since independent Screen children are automatically 32 apart. Lists alone have hairlines between peers. Cards hold decisions or the named item composition, never whole sections or nested cards. Photo/card/tag sizes come from tokens, with 44-point minimum touch targets; accessibility growth outranks clipping. Footer buttons are 48 high, with 16 plus the actual bottom safe area. Sources: `app/src/ui/layout.tsx:12`, `:122`; `brand/constitution.md:294` Articles 7 and 9; `product/frontend-standard.md:75` §§3 and 5.

Every pushed page has `chrome.tsx:34` `Nav back` and at most one subject action, followed by its one semantic title. The title and Nav scroll with the content today. There is no threshold, pinned compact title, shrink, downward hide or upward reveal. At top, both are visible; scrolling down takes them out naturally; scrolling up returns them only when their position reaches the viewport. A return restores the caller's item anchor, filter and offset. Root tabs share that behavior and keep their bar fixed. A native pinned/collapsing header is pending pattern 1, not an alternative each lane may choose. Sources: `objects.md` Shared behaviour and every scroll state; `lists.md` Shared state and navigation contract; `app/src/ui/layout.tsx:44`.

Use `Txt` roles through the kit. Short authored titles can use the display face; a user-entered object name, digits or a long heading uses the text-face role needed to read it whole. Do not force every title into Clofie 36 or one clipped line at large Dynamic Type. Sources: `brand/constitution.md:246` Article 6; `lists.md` §5; `product/frontend-standard.md:155` §5.
