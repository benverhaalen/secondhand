# What each pricing mode promises, and what supports its numbers

*Observed:* [Ben's full message](../../../product/words.md#2026-11-15-ben-pricing-modes-and-a-reason-for-every-number-backtests-wait-for-the-backend) asks for the purpose and reason behind each toggle. *Inferred:* this reference separates the existing calculations so a seller can understand the money and waiting involved. Proposed checks and display requirements below are *agent proposals*, not new decisions. No backtest was run for this document.

Its calculation does not apply the holding cost or minimum probability below. The table records both sources without treating them as one implemented policy.

## 2. What the toggle must show for each mode

*Inferred, agent proposal:* The complete journey is to open an item's Plan, see its saved choice or default `sell`, compare alternatives, then preview the listing. On each alternative, show the posted ask, likely accepted sale price, take-home after fees and seller shipping, median days to sale (`t50`), chance of sale within that alternative's horizon, and the downside against the adjacent choice. These must come from the same curve and [keep function](../../../app/src/engines/valuation/keep.ts#L62) used by the plan, with the same comp set, fee version, shipping assumptions and observation date. The numbers on the toggle must be the numbers the engine acts on.

*Observed:* [D13:414](../../determinations/money.md#d13-listing-price) distinguishes `start`, the target clearing rung, from `list`, the posted ask with learned offer room. [D11:375](../../determinations/money.md#d11-time-to-sell-range) defines t50 by where the sale curve reaches its midpoint; it is a median, not a mean or a promise. *Inferred, agent proposal:* Label the target sale price as a forecast, expose its uncertainty, and distinguish take-home conditional on a sale from probability-weighted expected take-home. Suppress unavailable forecasts rather than substituting sold-only average days.

| Mode | What the seller must see, using that shared calculation |
|---|---|
| *Inferred, agent proposal:* Price to sell. | Show the ask and target sale price, `keep(target)`, t50 and P at its quick horizon. Show dollars of expected take-home given up versus balanced and days potentially saved; do not assume either difference is positive. |
| *Inferred, agent proposal:* Price to the market. | Show those same fields at the balanced horizon. Against quick, show added waiting and expected take-home; against most money, show the further waiting and money trade. |
| *Inferred, agent proposal:* Most money. | Show those fields at the longest horizon. Against balanced, show added waiting, change in expected take-home and the risk of remaining unsold. A higher ask alone does not establish more money. |
| *Inferred, agent proposal:* Reserve. | Show the held ask, forecast sale price and conditional take-home if calculable. Label any probability and t50 as a forecast at that held price with an explicit forecast horizon, not an active markdown plan. Compare with most money to show the price of refusing drops; if a curve is missing, say the trade is unknown. |
| *Inferred, agent proposal:* Check. | Show the balanced calculation and explain that every offer waits for approval. Compare with balanced to isolate approval delay and any lost sale opportunities. The current curve cannot quantify a person's response delay, so this downside remains unknown until observed. |
| *Inferred, agent proposal:* Slider. | Recompute all fields at the chosen point and its neighbouring named marks. Keep the displayed probability period explicit while the slider still uses its fixed period. Report price, expected-money and t50 differences; a missing t50 remains missing. |

*Observed:* The app currently has the intent chips and editable ask in [plan.tsx:60](../../../app/src/app/item/[id]/plan.tsx#L60) and [plan.tsx:112](../../../app/src/app/item/[id]/plan.tsx#L112). Its default is the saved choice or `sell`. It shows “You keep up to” for quick, balanced and most money whenever a site keep is available. It shows an existing outlook's days range only while the choice equals the saved choice ([plan.tsx:94](../../../app/src/app/item/[id]/plan.tsx#L94)); reserve and check instead show explanatory text. Site rows show an ask-dependent keep through [model.ts:271](../../../app/src/item/model.ts#L271). These are displayed amounts, not demonstrated D10 forecasts for each alternative.

*Observed:* [model.ts:258](../../../app/src/item/model.ts#L258) chooses the range low for fast, the midpoint of range bounds for sell, and the high for best and check, with the floor enforced. It does not call `pricePath` or `slider`. The plan does not display a separate likely accepted sale price, per-mode calibrated t50, sale probability within each horizon, or dollar/day downside against neighbours. The posture engine returns `p_sale_30` and `days_t50` ([posture.ts:77](../../../app/src/engines/valuation/posture.ts#L68)), but no rendering reference to those fields was found in app TSX sources. The existing [RangeSlider](../../../app/src/ui/parts/range-slider.tsx#L22) is a buying price-range control used in [want.tsx](../../../app/src/app/want.tsx#L152) and the component gallery, not the seller posture slider. This is a source audit, not a demonstrated UI session.

## 5. How each mode is tested, planned only

*Observed:* [money-statistic.md](money-statistic.md#how-x-is-measured-on-data-with-unsold-listings) proposes money lift using NBER sold and unsold listings, then live confirmation; [datasets.md:46](datasets.md#2-what-each-dataset-can-prove-and-what-it-cannot) records their fields and limits. NBER belongs to Nate under [Ben's message](../../../product/words.md#2026-11-15-ben-pricing-modes-and-a-reason-for-every-number-backtests-wait-for-the-backend). StockX sold rows cannot test sale speed or the revenue caused by another asking price. *Inferred:* An observational fitted-curve comparison measures modelled policy value, while a causal claim needs the live matched experiment.

| Mode | Metric that says it did its job | Data and sample needed |
