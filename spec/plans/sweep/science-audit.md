# Science audit: how the engines determine and weight, and whether real-time gathering works

Read against the spec and code as they stood on 2026-11-15. Repo claims cite file and line. Method claims cite a named source. **(inferred)** marks a conclusion I reasoned to but did not observe directly. **(my arithmetic)** marks numbers I computed. The Wilson numbers were checked with a short node one-liner (z = 1.96), but no test was run. Engines marked *grep-only* were reviewed from a keyword sweep of their spec, not a full read; treat their rows as a first pass.

## Verdict

The methods the spec chooses are mostly the right ones from the literature: comparable sales with Kish effective sample size, credibility blending toward a parent, split and Mondrian conformal ranges, Wilson intervals, conjugate Beta and Gamma updates, Fellegi-Sunter linkage, hierarchical logistic models, and paired bootstrap promotion. But the specification of the core loop is not yet defensible as written. It contradicts itself on the two decisions that matter most to a person: whether a thin basis blends or backs off (spec/engines/valuation.md L60 vs L84), and whether a thin or wide number is shown or hidden (spec/product.md L594 vs spec/determinations/money.md L211, spec/engines/intelligence-api.md L257, app/src/engines/proof/proof.ts L135-139). Its calibration gate cannot be passed at the sample sizes it names (money.md L238 with L250-260, as coded in app/src/engines/valuation/check.ts L47-52). Its effective sample size cannot see how loose a match is, so a basis of near-misses counts the same as a basis of exact matches (app/src/engines/valuation/comps.ts L107-117, app/src/edge/intel/reconcile.ts L72). Real-time gathering is built and unit-tested as parts (gather.ts, learn.ts, guardWith, memoryProof). It is not wired: no production code calls `gather()`, writes an `observation` row, or feeds a gathered outcome back into a `param`. So the loop "person asks, thin basis, gather, better number, weight moves" does not run end to end today.

**Design judgement on gathering.**
- *Bias from looser matches.* The spec intends looser matches to count for less (`w_match`, money.md L54-86). That intent does not survive three boundaries: reconcile turns the label into a binary `in_basis` (reconcile.ts L72); gather's `measure()` gives every row full match weight (gather.ts L34-47); and Kish n_eff is scale-invariant, so even with `w_match` applied, ten 0.25-weight comps meet a basis of 6 (comps.ts L107-117). Each widening step therefore raises n_eff toward the basis using the rows most likely to be biased, and the stop rule then fires on exactly those rows. The range does not widen for match bias, because Q_g is fitted per group, not per `basis_level` (money.md L233) **(inferred)**.
- *Stopping rule.* "Median moved < 2% over the last 3" (gather.ts L43-45) is a data-dependent stop on the statistic being reported, the setting in which optional stopping biases the estimate and narrows apparent uncertainty (Armitage, McPherson & Rowe 1969). A pre-registered stop on n_eff (the D1 test) plus a cost or deadline is unbiased in the sense that matters here, because it does not look at the prices.
- *Honest uncertainty under a deadline.* The spec has a 120 s deadline (product.md L128) and 3 s / 20 s answer budgets (L572) but no rule for what range a partial gather shows. The defensible rule: show the range from whatever basis exists, with Q_g taken from the calibration cell for its `basis_level`, and label the level. Never shrink the range because the deadline came.

## Corrections, in order of consequence

1. **Wire the real-time loop, or stop claiming it.** *Changes:* product.md L541-556 and L572, intelligence-api.md §3.5. *Code:* check.ts, comps.ts L255, gather.ts, proof.ts, param.ts. *Wrong:* no production caller for `gather()`, the learn.ts functions or `guardWith`; no `observation` writer; no gathered outcome reaches a `param` (trace links 3-11). *Correct:* comps returns `basis_level` and a reason; check.ts raises `gather_request` on `too_few`; gather writes observations through a proof store; the answer cites observation ids; outcomes call `param.observe`. *Test:* one end-to-end test with a fixture site: a thin item yields `gather_request`, the gather writes N observations, the re-answer's `citation.basis_ids` equals those ids, `basis_level` moves up, and a `param` version increments.
2. **Make the basis see match looseness, and use one basis test everywhere.** *Changes:* money.md D1 L54-107, valuation.md L60, intelligence-api.md L252. *Code:* comps.ts L107-117, gather.ts L34-47, reconcile.ts L72. *Wrong:* scale-invariant Kish with mixed match levels; gather's own n_eff; binary `in_basis`. *Correct:* carry the match label as `w_match`. Calibrate Q_g per `basis_level` (Mondrian on level; Vovk et al. 2005). Gather stops on comps' own `sufficient()`. *Test:* backtest coverage stratified by `basis_level`, each level within its band; a unit test that ten family-level comps do not meet the basis.
3. **Replace the coverage gate with one that can be passed.** *Changes:* money.md D6 L238, L250-260, L904; valuation.md L190. *Code:* check.ts L47-52, backtest.py L121-140. *Wrong (my arithmetic):* with Checked = Wilson 95% lower bound ≥ 0.70 and pass = coverage in [0.75, 0.85], as check.ts L47 conjoins them, no n from 30 to 38 can satisfy both (at n = 30, LB ≥ 0.70 needs 26/30 = 0.867 > 0.85). With true coverage exactly 0.80 the probability of passing is about 13% at n = 39, 12% at n = 50 and 57% at n = 100, before "every cell" multiplies these across cells. ## What is sound and should be defended as is

- **Kish n_eff as the measure of weight dispersion.** proof.ts L80-98 and comps.ts L107-117 normalize by the max, which is correct and numerically safe (Kish 1965). The fix in corr. 2 is what it is applied to, not the formula.
- **Split and Mondrian conformal with uniform weights,** as in jobs/valuation/market.py L322 (Vovk et al. 2005; Lei et al. 2018), with rolling-origin backtests, a 7-day gap and leakage removal (money.md L236).
- **The Wilson interval** for coverage and precision (backtest.py L103-108; identity.md L74), the right small-sample interval for a proportion (Brown, Cai & DasGupta 2001).
- **Paired bootstrap resampled by product** in jobs/valuation/experiment.py L332 with 1,000 draws (L54-55): the correct unit of resampling for clustered comps (Field & Welsh 2007).
- **Censoring kept in D10** (money.md L351), cross-site sales censored rather than counted unsold (platform-analytics.md L39), with IPCW calibration (Gerds & Schumacher 2006).
- **Fellegi-Sunter with Splink EM and Beta priors** for identity (identity.md L45-74; Fellegi & Sunter 1969).
- **Hierarchical logistic with partial pooling and Thompson sampling** in negotiation (negotiation.md L56-68; Gelman & Hill 2007; Chapelle & Li 2011), and **Gamma-Poisson observed-over-expected shrinkage** in platform analytics (platform-analytics.md L40; Clayton & Kaldor 1987), with its seeded mix-control test (L115).
- **Conjugate Beta and Gamma updates with lineage that climbs only** (param.ts L40-56), **frozen on an adapter bump** and **one-UPDATE rollback** (product.md L553-554): simple, auditable, and correct once corr. 11-12 land.
- **Append-only observations and content-addressed citations** (proof.ts L58-78, L102-114, L158-176): the citation id is a hash of the definition, versions and basis, so a number can be recomputed and checked (the kill test, product.md L594).
- **Numeric conflicts by weighted median with every value kept** (reconcile.ts L132-139): robust to one wrong route, and never invents a value (reconcileText L162-163).
