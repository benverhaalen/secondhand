# Buy, round one: the merge

Three designs, each for one person in [product/personas.md](../../../../product/personas.md). The critic's full read is in [critique.json](critique.json). buy-hunting and buy-price passed; buy-browsing failed on text too small to read.

**Base: buy-hunting.** It is the only one built on a person with any evidence behind them, and it shows work done for the person.

| Keep | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shelves New finds, Watching, Bought, new match first | hunting | Matches spec/features/buy.md |
| One field for words or a link, camera inside it | price | Three entry controls become one |
| Two-word price read with an icon, asking and fair range both delivered | price | Everyone benefits from the read |
| One line saying the search is still running | hunting | Saves a return check |

**Left out:** the browsing feed (buy-browsing). The person is unsourced and the feed pushes the saved search down. *Agent proposal:* one setting, off by default, later.

**Fixes for round two:** say the news once (round one says it with a heading, a bell and a New pill), lighter type and more room, "View listing" when the button opens the marketplace.

Not checked by anyone: the prices, the fair ranges and the coverage count are invented for the mockup.
