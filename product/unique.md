# What we did that we haven't seen anywhere else

Ben, 06:44: "did you figure out any super unique ways that we ended up solving problems. ways that nobody really has done things before or a super unique way and why and how to exemplify that in the pitch or website etc" ([words](words.md#2026-11-15-what-did-we-do-that-nobody-else-has)). *Agent proposal*.

Before calling anything new, we checked who already does it: the crosslisters (Vendoo, Crosslist, List Perfectly, PrimeLister, Nifty), the price-check apps (FlipTip, Underpriced, Flipper Vintage), the marketplaces' own AI, and Photoroom for photos. The honest wording is "we haven't seen another tool that does this", never "first in the world". Each row says what we checked and what we found.

**Not new, so don't pitch it as new:** showing a price range (Zillow, FlipTip and Kelley Blue Book do it); recommending where to list (Flipper Vintage and FlipTip say "best place to list"); AI listing writing (everyone). Our versions are careful (a range that hides itself with its reason when the data is thin; a site verdict that says why the couch doesn't go on Depop), but say "done honestly", not "new".

## How to show it

- **Pitch:** after the demo, one line per item for the top three, each with its 5-second proof. "Three things we haven't seen anyone else do."
- **Devpost:** a "What's new here" section with the top three, one sentence each, with the number.
- **Website:** a "How it's different" band with three cards, each a picture and one line: the counter running to 931,406; the photo with the held box; the reply with the flagged claim. Wording handed to the website thread.
- **At the table:** if a judge says "Vendoo does this", answer with the row's "what others do" column: what they do, what we do differently, and how we checked.
