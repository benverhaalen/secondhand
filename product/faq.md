## Selling

**What does secondhand do?**
Take a few photos of something you own. We work out what it is and what it's worth, list it on the sites where it will sell, answer buyers, and take it down everywhere when it sells.

**Which sites?**
eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Depop, Poshmark and Mercari. We also check prices on OfferUp and Grailed.

**Will it list my things everywhere?**
Only where they'll sell. A sofa goes to Facebook Marketplace, not Depop, and we say why. You can add a site we'd skip, unless the site doesn't allow it.

**Will it sell below my price?**
Never. You set the lowest you'd take. Anything under it waits for you.

**What does it do without asking me?**
Answers questions your listing already answers, takes listings down after a sale, and handles offers inside your limits. Each one is marked "Sent for you" and has a Why?.

**What always waits for me?**
The first post of each item, any price under your minimum, refunds, your address, and anything that moves a deal off the site.

**Can I approve every message myself?**
Yes. Choose Answer for me, Draft for me, or Off, for all sites or one. Drafts wait with the exact words, and you send, edit or take over the chat.

**How do you know it sold?**
We check each site for a paid order, every one to two minutes while you have listings up. When one sells, the others come down. If one doesn't, it turns red so you can fix it.

**What if it sells on two sites at once?**
You get one card with both buyers, what you'd keep from each, and what cancelling each would cost. One tap cancels the other with a polite note.

**Can I take something down myself?**
Any time. Sold it to a friend, kept it, gave it away: one tap ends every listing.

**I'm going away. Can I pause?**
Pause selling, with an end date. We use each site's own away mode where it has one.

**Can I see everything that happened?**
Every action on every site is in Activity, with a link to the same thing on the site.

## Photos

**Do you change my photos?**
Only if you ask. Clean up fixes light, angle and crop on your own photo. Enhance with AI can redraw light and creases, and is off until you turn it on.

**How do I know an AI photo is honest?**
We compare every AI photo with your original before you see it. If a flaw disappears, the tag text changes, or the color or shape drifts, we throw it out. Your real photo is always first on every site, and AI photos are labeled.

**Can I hide a stain?**
No. A hidden flaw comes back as a return. You choose how it's described, and it's shown.

## Meeting up

**Where should I meet a buyer?**
A public place you save once. If your police department has a Safe Exchange Zone, it's a good default. Daylight hours.

**Will you give out my address?**
Never on our own. Your address goes out only when you tap Share address, each time.

**How are meetups scheduled?** **(launch)**
We offer the buyer two times from your free time that day. You approve the message, they pick one, and it goes on your calendar. We check with them before you leave.

**What should I watch for?**
Someone who changes the place at the last minute, asks to pay outside the site, sends an "overpayment", or asks for a code. We flag these and send nothing until you decide.

## Money

**Do you hold my money?**
No. Buyers pay on the site, and the site pays you.

**What will I keep?**
We show what lands in your account on each site, after that site's fees, before you list.

**Who handles refunds and returns?**
The site, under its own rules. We show you the deadline and put together your photos and listing as evidence. A refund always waits for your tap.

Not set yet.

## Your accounts

**Do you see my passwords?**
eBay uses its own sign-in. For the others you sign in on the site's real login page, and we keep the session, not the password, unless you choose to save it.

**Could my account get banned?**
Every site has its own rules about automation. We act only on your account, after your approval, at a human pace, and we stop on any site that sends a warning. We show the risk for each site before you link it.

**Can I unlink a site or delete everything?**
Yes, any time, from You.

## Buying

**What does Buy do?**
One search across every site and official stores. Type it, photograph it, or paste a link. We tell you what it is, what it usually sells for, and the total with shipping.

**Will you buy things for me?**
No. We find it, draft offers and questions for you to approve, and send you to the site to pay.

**Is the cheapest listing ranked first because you're paid?**
No. Referral links never change the ranking.

**What if it's fake or not as described?**
We show each site's buyer protection on the listing. A Facebook pickup has none; we say so before you go.
