# The submission video

*Agent proposal.* `secondhand-video.mp4` is the current cut: 1080p, about 1:34, dark brand theme, the wordmark in Clofie and everything else in PP Neue Montreal.

- `timeline.json` is the script: each scene's start, length and narration line.
- `render.py` draws the picture from the app screenshots. It reads them from `work/devpost/screens/`, which is not in the repo, so it runs only on Ben's Mac.
- The narration is Ben's voice, generated on his Mac from a recording he made. The recording, the voice model and the generated lines are not in the repo.
- The screens are the current build showing Ben's own Depop listings as the data.
