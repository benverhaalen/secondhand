# Who the designs lean toward

Six people, each defined by what they want from one visit, not by age or job. The image loop leans each first-round design toward one of them ([frontend-standard §2](frontend-standard.md#personas-for-the-first-round)). The set is an *agent proposal*. It comes from [the first person we build for](decisions.md#2026-11-14-the-first-person-we-build-for-is-someone-clearing-their-own-things), from [what sellers and buyers want](../research/cross-cutting/ben/user-wants.md), and from the three selling intents Ben named ([words](words.md#2026-11-15-the-new-button-gradients-that-carry-state-seller-intents-onboarding-and-build-ready)).

| Person | Their situation | What they want | What they fear | The line a design call gets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clearing out | Moving, or tired of the pile. Many cheap things | The things gone. Money comes second | Messages, no-shows, a chore that never ends | Design this for someone who wants a pile of their own things gone this week and would rather give one away than answer ten messages about it |
| Holding a minimum | One thing worth real money, and a lowest price in mind | The most they keep, with no surprise below their minimum | Being talked down, or the app dropping the price for them | Design this for someone selling one thing they know the worth of, who will wait for their price and wants to see at once that nothing went below it |
| Fire sale | Needs the money or the space now | It sold today | Time passing with nothing happening | Design this for someone who needs this sold today and will take less to get it done |
| Testing the water | Likes the thing. Would sell only for the right price | To learn what it would fetch, and keep it otherwise | Selling by accident, or being pushed to list | Design this for someone who would keep this unless the price is right, and mostly wants to know what it would fetch |
| Hunting one thing | Knows what they want to buy, in their size and budget | It found and held, with one tap to buy | Missing it, or buying a listing that was already gone | Design this for someone looking for one specific thing used, who wants to be told when it turns up and not to search again |
| Reseller | Dozens of live items across sites | Speed across many items, never a double sale | Slow bulk work | Used only as a stress case: check the design holds with 200 items. A design never leans toward them |

## Using them

- **Take two to four per screen**, the ones who use that screen. Capture and Sell lean toward the four sellers. Buy leans toward the hunter. Today takes whoever has something waiting.
- **The line gives the situation, never the layout.** It says who is looking and what they want, so the model works out the screen.
- **When two people's wants conflict**, the one clearing out wins, because they are the first person we build for. Better still, turn the conflict into something the person sets once, such as their minimum or "sell it fast".
- **Add a person only when a real one shows up** in the demo or in research who wants something none of these do.
