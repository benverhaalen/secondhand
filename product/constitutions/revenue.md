# The revenue constitution

How a free app pays, and the numbers we show for it. The argument is in [business.md](../business.md) and the market in [pitch/market.md](../pitch/market.md); the per-feature numbers come from the thread "Quantify every feature with why and ROI". *Agent proposal*.

## The problem

A judge will ask how a free app makes money, and whether that is honest. Sellers won't pay before they've sold, and the crosslisters they already know charge monthly. Ben's position is that free is the viable model "because of all the other business models" ([05:43](../words.md#2026-11-15-both-sides-in-the-demo-why-people-want-it-and-free-as-the-business-model)), with referral revenue when a buyer clicks through to a listing and, per Nate, anonymous market data ([words](../words.md#2026-11-14-nate-referrals-and-market-data)). The job is to show what each item costs us to run, what each revenue stream earns per item, and the return at scale, from research anyone can inspect, and to say plainly where it doesn't work.

**Success, checkably:** cost per listed item and per watched want, measured from our own logs; revenue per stream with its source; one honest scale scenario; the hurdles named.

## Rules

**R1. Free to the person.** No subscription, no fee on a sale ([05:43](../words.md#2026-11-15-both-sides-in-the-demo-why-people-want-it-and-free-as-the-business-model)).

**R2. Every stream is named, sized and sourced.** Buy-side referral links, sized against what referral businesses like Honey earn ([08:09](../words.md#2026-11-15-quantify-everything-a-why-for-every-rule-ranked-identify-and-revenue-streams)); aggregated anonymous market data (Nate); partnerships with marketplaces only as a question ([06:10](../words.md#2026-11-15-adjacent-appraisal-risk-and-why-this-price): "im not really sure to be honest").

**R3. Costs come from our logs, not guesses.** Model calls, image runs and cloud browser minutes per item, from [log/](../../log/README.md) ([08:09](../words.md#2026-11-15-quantify-everything-a-why-for-every-rule-ranked-identify-and-revenue-streams): "look at our costs and estimate an roi for everything at scale").

**R4. Flattering hypotheticals, inspectable sources.** "use hypotheticals backed in research but research that flatters us but it honest and inspectable" ([08:09](../words.md#2026-11-15-quantify-everything-a-why-for-every-rule-ranked-identify-and-revenue-streams)). Every figure links to where it came from and carries its mark.

**R5. Data is sold only aggregated and anonymous.** Never a person's listings, messages or sign-ins; a person can see and clear what was learned ([ways out, 8](../ui-constitution.md#article-6-complete-not-minimal-every-automatic-step-has-a-way-around-it)); third-party AI providers never see credentials ([06:40](../words.md#2026-11-15-its-always-you-security-open-source-and-show-it-working-first)).

**R6. Say where it doesn't work.** Where we don't reach, what has to be true, and the hurdles, "keeping it extremely honest" ([05:43](../words.md#2026-11-15-both-sides-in-the-demo-why-people-want-it-and-free-as-the-business-model); [business.md](../business.md#the-hurdles-plainly)).

**R7. Impact claims are cited or cut.** What secondhand does for the earth is said only with a cited figure ([06:10](../words.md#2026-11-15-adjacent-appraisal-risk-and-why-this-price)).
