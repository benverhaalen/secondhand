Simple Icons ships monochrome single-path marks. The logos remain trademarks of their owners and are used only to say where an item is listed. Use them unaltered (no recoloring beyond the brand color, no stretching, no letter substitutes), and follow each brand's guidelines.

Usage: on a photo, put the logo in an 18 px white bubble at the bottom right. In rows and labels, use the bare logo at 15 to 20 px.
