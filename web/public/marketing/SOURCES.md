# Landing page image sources

The wall uses a selection of the repository's existing photo-wall collection in `brand/explore/web-home/photos/wall/tiles/`. Original attribution is maintained with that collection. The selection is recorded in `web/components/marketing/wall-photos.ts`.

Marketplace marks are unchanged copies from `assets/platforms/square/`. The paired-tag logo is the existing `web/public/brand/mark.svg`.

The phone images show an app build from November 15, 2026. The capture checkout was `b7a60fdd`, combining the current layout at `a04973f2` with the typography change at `c7f0c312`. The phone shell frames complete app captures.

The Carhartt jacket and Kindle are demo items. The jacket image also appears on What’s Next. See the [demo image provenance](../../../assets/demo-items/README.md). The listed workflows show different stages of the app, and the Kindle sale is separate from the jacket example. The historical pricing chart comes from `scripts/bench/out/backtest-stockx.json`.
