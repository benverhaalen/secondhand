# Landing page image sources

The wall uses a selection of the repository's existing photo-wall collection in `brand/explore/web-home/photos/wall/tiles/`. Original attribution is maintained with that collection. The selection is recorded in `web/components/marketing/wall-photos.ts`.

Marketplace marks are unchanged copies from `assets/platforms/square/`. The paired-tag logo is the existing `web/public/brand/mark.svg`.

The phone images were captured on November 15, 2026 from real app screens, with sample data and the actual gold Marquette tee from `assets/demo-items/real/marquette-tee/01_cover.jpg`. They are not AI-generated interfaces. The device shell is presentation framing around the complete viewport.

| Asset | App source |
| --- | --- |
| capture.png | Camera in the stage fixture, with the real tee as the camera feed |
| preview.png | Post preview for it_stage, with one photo in the isolated browser fixture and AI photo editing off |
| plan.png | Plan for it_stage in the stage fixture |
| watch.png | Want screen with the Brewers jersey search in the clearing fixture |
| ship.png | Ship for it_tee in the clearing fixture |

Displayed prices, buyers, transactions, comparable sales and shipping details are sample data. The homepage labels the screenshots accordingly. Plan and Review are different fixture states and do not document a completed end-to-end pricing interaction. The two How It Works screens show the same tee. The historical pricing chart is sourced separately from `scripts/bench/out/backtest-stockx.json`.
